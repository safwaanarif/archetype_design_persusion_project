# The Caregiver

## What It Is

The Caregiver archetype represents compassion, generosity, protection, and helping others. Caregiver brands focus on supporting people and making their lives safer, healthier, or easier.

## When to Use It

The Caregiver archetype works well for brands connected to healthcare, education, charities, childcare, community services, and products that help people. It is useful when a brand wants to communicate trust, support, and compassion.

## How to Apply It

### Imagery

Use images of people helping each other, families, communities, healthcare workers, teachers, and acts of kindness.

### Colors

Soft blue, green, white, and other calming colors can communicate care, safety, and trust.

### Typography

Use clean, friendly, and easy-to-read fonts that create a welcoming feeling.

### Wording

Use supportive, caring, and reassuring language. Examples include:

- "Here to help."
- "Care when you need it."
- "Supporting you every step of the way."

## Examples

A healthcare organization could use the Caregiver archetype by showing healthcare professionals helping patients and communicating that people are supported and cared for.
