# The Artist

## What It Is

The Artist archetype represents creativity, self-expression, originality, imagination, and the desire to create something meaningful. Artist brands encourage people to express themselves and appreciate unique ideas and designs.

## When to Use It

The Artist archetype works well for brands connected to art, design, music, photography, fashion, writing, and other creative fields. It is useful when a brand wants to emphasize originality and personal expression.

## How to Apply It

### Imagery

Use artwork, creative designs, photography, illustrations, music, and images of people expressing themselves.

### Colors

Creative and expressive color combinations can support the Artist identity. The colors can be bold, subtle, or artistic depending on the brand.

### Typography

Use distinctive and creative fonts while making sure the text remains easy to read.

### Wording

Use expressive and inspirational language. Examples include:

- "Express your creativity."
- "Create something unique."
- "Your ideas. Your expression."

## Examples

An art supply company could use the Artist archetype by showing people creating paintings, drawings, or other artwork. A music brand could also use this archetype by focusing on creativity and self-expression.

## Sources

- Carl Jung's work on archetypes provides background for understanding archetypal concepts.
- Mark and Pearson's *The Hero and the Outlaw* discusses the major brand archetypes.
