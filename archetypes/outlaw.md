# The Outlaw

## What It Is

The Outlaw archetype represents freedom, rebellion, independence, and challenging the status quo. Outlaw brands often encourage people to question traditional expectations and create their own rules.

## When to Use It

The Outlaw archetype works well for brands that want to challenge conventions, stand out from competitors, or appeal to people who value independence. It can be used in industries such as fashion, entertainment, technology, and lifestyle.

## How to Apply It

### Imagery

Use bold and unconventional imagery. Images can show individuality, rebellion, street culture, alternative lifestyles, or people breaking away from traditional expectations.

### Colors

Dark or bold colors such as black, red, gray, and other strong contrasting colors can support the Outlaw identity.

### Typography

Use bold, distinctive, and unconventional fonts that communicate independence and confidence.

### Wording

Use direct and rebellious language. Examples include:

- "Break the rules."
- "Do things differently."
- "Create your own path."

## Examples

A clothing brand could use the Outlaw archetype by using unconventional designs and advertising that encourages people to express their individuality rather than follow trends.
