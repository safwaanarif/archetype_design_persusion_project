# The Ruler

## What It Is

The Ruler archetype represents leadership, responsibility, control, stability, and success. Ruler brands communicate confidence, organization, authority, and a desire to create order.

## When to Use It

The Ruler archetype works well for brands connected to business, finance, luxury, leadership, and professional services. It is useful when a brand wants to communicate reliability, status, and leadership.

## How to Apply It

### Imagery

Use professional images of leaders, successful businesses, organized environments, formal settings, and high-quality products.

### Colors

Black, dark blue, gold, gray, and other sophisticated colors can support the Ruler identity.

### Typography

Use professional, strong, and elegant fonts that communicate confidence and authority.

### Wording

Use confident and professional language. Examples include:

- "Lead with confidence."
- "Built for those who lead."
- "Set the standard."

## Examples

A financial services company could use the Ruler archetype by presenting itself as reliable, professional, and experienced. A luxury brand could also use this archetype to communicate quality, status, and exclusivity.
