# The Innocent

## What It Is

The Innocent archetype represents optimism, simplicity, honesty, and a desire for safety and happiness. Brands using this archetype often communicate a positive and trustworthy personality. They focus on making people feel comfortable, hopeful, and secure.

## When to Use It

The Innocent archetype is useful for brands that want to create feelings of trust, comfort, positivity, and simplicity. It can work well for products and services connected to family, wellness, education, food, and everyday happiness.

## How to Apply It

### Imagery

Use bright, simple, and welcoming imagery. Images can show nature, families, friendship, happiness, or peaceful everyday moments.

### Colors

Common colors include white, light blue, soft green, and other simple or calming colors.

### Typography

Use clean, simple, and easy-to-read fonts. Avoid overly complicated or aggressive typography.

### Wording

Use positive, friendly, and reassuring language. Examples include:

- "Simple solutions for everyday life."
- "Helping you feel good every day."
- "A simpler way to live."

## Examples

A brand using the Innocent archetype might focus on simplicity, honesty, happiness, and making customers feel comfortable. For example, a family-oriented product could use friendly imagery and reassuring language to create a sense of trust.
