## Cialdini's Persuasion Principles

### 1. Commitment and Consistency
I can show commitment by consistently working toward my goals and improving my skills. For example, I can share my progress in school, computer science, and athletics.

### 2. Social Proof
I can use social proof by showing accomplishments, positive feedback, teamwork, and examples of projects I have completed.

### 3. Authority
I can demonstrate authority by showing my computer science projects, skills, education, and knowledge that I develop over time.

### 4. Liking
I can make my personal brand more relatable by showing my personality, interests, and involvement in soccer and other activities.

### 5. Reciprocity
I can provide useful information or help to classmates and teammates. Sharing what I learn can encourage others to connect with me.

### 6. Scarcity
I can highlight unique skills, experiences, or projects that make my personal brand different from others.
