# The Hero

## What It Is

The Hero archetype represents courage, determination, achievement, discipline, and the desire to overcome challenges. Hero brands inspire people to work hard, improve themselves, and accomplish difficult goals.

## When to Use It

The Hero archetype works well for brands connected to sports, fitness, education, technology, competition, and personal achievement. It is useful when a brand wants to motivate people and show strength or determination.

## How to Apply It

### Imagery

Use images of athletes, competitions, achievements, challenges, teamwork, and people overcoming obstacles.

### Colors

Strong and energetic colors such as red, black, white, and dark blue can support the Hero identity.

### Typography

Use bold, strong, and modern fonts that communicate confidence and determination.

### Wording

Use motivational and action-oriented language. Examples include:

- "Push yourself further."
- "Overcome every challenge."
- "Be ready to achieve more."

## Examples

A sports brand could use the Hero archetype by showing athletes training and competing. The brand could encourage customers to work hard, overcome challenges, and reach their goals.
