# The Creator

## What It Is

The Creator archetype represents creativity, imagination, self-expression, and the desire to create something meaningful and original. Creator brands encourage people to use their imagination and turn ideas into reality.

## When to Use It

The Creator archetype works well for brands connected to art, design, writing, music, technology, photography, and other creative activities. It is useful when a brand wants to emphasize originality and self-expression.

## How to Apply It

### Imagery

Use images of artwork, designs, creative projects, tools, technology, and people creating something.

### Colors

Creative brands can use a wide range of colors. Bold or artistic color combinations can communicate imagination and originality.

### Typography

Use distinctive, creative, or modern fonts while keeping the text easy to read.

### Wording

Use language that encourages creativity and self-expression. Examples include:

- "Create something original."
- "Turn your ideas into reality."
- "Make something that is uniquely yours."

## Examples

A design software company could use the Creator archetype by showing designers creating artwork and turning their ideas into finished projects. An art supply brand could also encourage customers to express themselves through creativity.
